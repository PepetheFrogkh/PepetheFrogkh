## How to write an article

One `.mdx` file per article. The front matter, between the two dashed lines,
carries the title, the summary, the date, the topics and the draft flag. The
rest is Markdown.

A draft, `draft: true`, shows up neither in the list, nor in the RSS feed, nor
in the sitemap, and its address answers 404.

## What is picked up automatically

Reading time is worked out from the word count. The topics in the front matter
feed the filters on the list page. Nothing to declare anywhere else.
